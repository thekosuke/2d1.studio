# Finds imagery — resolution and warm treatment, 10 October 2026

All 55 active canvas images use transparent square frames with consistent optical
weight. Isabelle is removed from both language routes; its entry and translation
are archived in `_archive/removed-isabelle-2026-10-10/`. The pre-round catalog and
replaced gallery sources are in `_archive/pre-finds-warm-2026-10-10/`.

## Photography treatment

A shared CSS grade in `css/objects.css` gently warms highlights, reduces saturation
by 2%, and softens contrast by 1.5%: `sepia(.055) saturate(.98) contrast(.985)`.
It applies only to canvas, fallback and product-gallery images. The source pixels
remain intact, so the grade is reversible; it does not tint UI or flatten alpha.
No generated detail, artificial enlargement, stretch, added shadow, or grain.

Cutouts retain original photo RGB and native source alpha, or local Apple Vision
foreground masks with manual silhouette corrections. HAY press originals are
converted from their embedded profile to sRGB before export. Optical fit remains
73–90% depending on silhouette, 87% for collections and 72% for app icons.

## Resolution audit

The references (curated.supply and goods.so) commonly deliver roughly 1,200–1,500px
product images, with larger originals on some entries. Judge usable product pixels,
not padding. This round replaces 12 main-image sources and upgrades 31 gallery
photos. Original sources are linked below and individually credited in the UI.

HAY’s original Miz and Regn photographs are 5,250×3,500 and 5,700×3,800. The
RICOH source is 3,698×2,601; Nintendo’s unobstructed console view is 3,000×1,100.
The two app icons now use official 1,024px App Store artwork instead of small
website vignettes. Their opaque corner backing is removed along the icon outline.
Gallery originals are exported at up to 3,200px long edge, without enlargement.
Masters keep full native subject detail; 480/960/1440/1920px responsive variants
are provided only when smaller than the master. Canvas `sizes` follows zoom.

This is not a claim that every source meets the reference resolution. Eight
subjects remain below 800px on their long edge after checking manufacturer,
retailer and designer/press sources: Twergi, HomeinFun, Peepers, Camera Art Model,
Color Picker sticker, Gift&Stock, nendo shoe-horn, and First Light Snoopy.
The sticker and Gift&Stock remain the clearest resolution limitations. Larger
files found for several of these only add background, crop the object, or hide
it behind props. Keep the verified images until genuinely sharper photos exist.
No blurred derivative is presented as an upgraded original.

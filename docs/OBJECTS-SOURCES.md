All 25 original selections are included, plus 31 additional selections (56 total). The owner’s reference identified Mr. CONTAC; a matching higher-resolution source photo replaces the deleted Mercari listing. See the additions below.

Selection notes:
- Twergi and Tinned Goods name collections. One representative is used: the yellow Alessi ES19 mill and Block Design Play tin. The owner can choose a different member.
- The Anna reference is the black AAM01 B version, not the previous chrome placeholder. It has a new stable ID (`alessi-anna-g-black`); old ownership marks are never reassigned. HAY Miz retains its ID. All other selections use new IDs.
- The unnamed Instagram references resolve to Robi Boris, Nuutajärvi Tumbler 1709, Seiko W286, Girard Throw, and HAY Regn Umbrella.
- The supplied “Sun Clock” URL identifies the blue/red Everybody Wall Clock; use that product rather than a similarly named variant.
- Isabelle is the Re-Ment Happy Room miniature licensed from Nintendo. The Bruna rabbit is an ides × MEDICOM TOY × BEAMS collaboration, based on Dick Bruna’s character.
- Source photography is saved locally, with original file dimensions below. Square presentation is controlled by the existing CSS; the source files are not cropped. Instagram links remain as provenance for vintage/brand-post images; their transient CDN links below document the download, not a runtime dependency.
- Private preview only. No public-use image license or production publication is implied. No hosted ownership data was changed.

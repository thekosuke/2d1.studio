Privacy

Updated October 30, 2026

Who we are

2D1 is the design practice of Kosuke Futsukaichi. For questions about your information, contact kosuke@2d1.studio.

When you get in touch

We use your name, email, optional company name, inquiry category and message to respond to you, discuss potential work and manage any resulting project. The page and language you use, submission time and a technical reference help us handle the inquiry and avoid duplicate submissions.

Inquiries accepted through the form are stored in Notion. Email notifications and replies use our Google Workspace mailbox. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes requests and the technical records needed to run and protect the website. These providers may process information outside your country.

We do not use inquiry details for marketing without a separate choice, and we do not sell them. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in your message.

Keeping and removing information

For inquiries that do not lead to a project, we keep correspondence for 12 months after our last meaningful contact, then delete it. Records needed for ongoing projects or applicable recordkeeping requirements may be kept longer. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion; we may need to confirm that the request is yours.

The website does not save unfinished inquiry messages in browser storage. Small server records help prevent duplicate submissions without storing the message text there. Request metadata may also be processed for security and abuse prevention.

Collection preferences and Purchased marks

Collection preferences are saved in this browser and do not sync across devices. Clearing site data removes them. Purchased marks use a random first-party browser identifier in a cookie that can last up to one year, alongside your selected product marks on our server. No account is required.

You can undo a Purchased mark on its item. Clearing the cookie disconnects this browser from its existing marks; it does not automatically remove those server records.

Optional visit analytics

Google Analytics loads only if you allow it below. It helps us understand visits and how the website is used. We do not send inquiry field contents to analytics, and our page-view events omit URL query strings and fragments. Advertising personalization is disabled. Your browser’s Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal keeps analytics off.

Analytics is off. The website works the same either way.

Your choice is saved in this browser when browser storage is available. Turning it off stops our analytics measurement and removes the Google Analytics cookies accessible to this website. It does not erase information already received by Google. See Google’s analytics privacy information.

Your questions

For privacy questions or requests, email kosuke@2d1.studio. If you follow a product or social link, that website handles your information under its own policy.

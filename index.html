Meet Character 01.

Original objects for everyday life, made under our own name. The first one is a tote bag.

Stitched, not printed. And it blinks.

Built square. Built to be carried every day.

Nothing outside. Everything inside.

Character 01

Tote Bag

Each one starts with a little character and hopes to become someone’s favorite something.

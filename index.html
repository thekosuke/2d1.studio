2D1 is an independent design house working across brands, digital products, and ideas. We help shape things from early thinking through to how they look, work, and live in the world.

Studio

Clear ideas, with character.

At our core, we’re a design studio. We work with small teams and businesses to make their ideas clear and give them character. We strip each idea down to its essence, keeping the one detail that makes you smile.

Sometimes that means building a brand from the beginning. Sometimes it means rethinking a website, refining a product, or finding a better way to tell an existing story. From strategy and visual identity down to the pixels of an interface, we bring a fresh pair of eyes and a hands-on approach.

The aim is to create something that is clear, intuitive, and uniquely you.

Shop

Familiar characters in our everyday lives.

The joy of making is at the heart of what we do. The things we make for ourselves start the same way as our client work: with a clear idea and a detail that makes them their own.

The tote bag is our first everyday item: a canvas tote made to tag along wherever you go. A simple shape, handy inner pockets, and a flash of red so you know it’s yours. We’re working through the details, from how the strap feels in your hand to where the small things go inside.

The bag is still in development. If you’d like it to become part of your day, sign up for updates.

Material
Tightly woven black canvas.
Dimensions
Approx. W 40 × H 36 × D 10 cm, excluding the strap.
Strap
One long strap, black outside and red inside. For shoulder carry.
Red tag
A square woven label, folded into the side seam.
Pockets
A bottle-holding band and a square pocket for small items.
Opening
Magnetic closure sewn into the top hem.
Details
Subtle charcoal pixel eyes on the front; a small embroidered 2D1 logo centered near the top of the back.

Prototype specifications. Dimensions and details may change before production.

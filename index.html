Specifications

Material
Armada No. 8 cotton canvas

Armada is Senda’s 100% cotton No. 8 canvas, woven in Japan. Its coarse weave and firm, starched finish give the bag structure, then gradually soften with use. We wanted a bag that holds its shape and gains character as it becomes part of your day.

Where it comes from mattered just as much. We also considered Alberton, which brings historic American textiles back to life in Japan. Armada felt closer to the story we wanted to tell: an everyday object made with Japanese canvas. Making things in Japan is an important part of 2D1, and that choice starts with the fabric.

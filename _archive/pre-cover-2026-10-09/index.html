Studio

Clear ideas, with character.

We work by editing until the idea is clear, then keeping the one detail that makes you smile. We work with founders, small teams, and independent businesses to make their ideas clear and give them a character of their own. Sometimes that means building a brand from the beginning. Sometimes it means rethinking a website, refining a product, or finding a better way to tell an existing story. From strategy and visual identity to the details of a digital experience, we bring a fresh pair of eyes and a hands-on approach. Familiar Characters

Character 01 is our first everyday object: a canvas tote made to find a place in your routine. A familiar shape, a long strap, and a flash of red where you might not expect it. We’re working through the details, from how it sits on your shoulder to where the small things go inside. Like the work we do for others, it starts with a clear idea and keeps the detail that makes it its own. The bag is still in development. If you’d like to see it become a familiar part of your day, let us know.

Material
Tightly woven black canvas.
Dimensions
Approx. W 38 × H 38 × D 10 cm, excluding the strap.
Strap
One long strap, black outside and red inside. For shoulder or crossbody carry.
Red tag
A square woven label, folded into the side seam.
Interior
A bottle-holding band and a square pocket for keys, earbuds, and other small things.
Opening
Open top, with a folded and stitched hem.
Details
Subtle charcoal pixel eyes on the front; a small embroidered 2D1 logo at the top-center of the back.

Prototype specifications. Dimensions and details may change before production.

Relationship Advice

Culturally, people are tired of dating apps. For those who seek love but cannot find the person that’s right for them, there are other services such as matchmaking services and marriage agencies. I personally enjoy watching dating reality TV shows. They might not be 100% authentic, but they do show how people approach dating and what people value in modern romantic relationships.

After consuming all this content, I noticed these services tend to be offered by woman. The advisor tends to be woman. In the show, Indian Matchmaking, the advisor is a woman. In the film, Materialists, the matchmaker acted by Dakota Johnson is a woman. In Japan, the popular founder of the marriage agency IBJ is a woman.

As a heterosexual male who lacks the traditional macho values and has always leaned more towards feminine sensitivities, I found it easier to have conversations with woman rather than men. With this, I felt like I can offer a male perspective that are rooted in my past interactions and experience with people, that my female counterparts might not be able to.

Disclaimer that I’m no professional. This is completely free, pro-bono work.

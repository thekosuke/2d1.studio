A tray is a small piece of architecture: it makes a room within a room. This one is a single pressed sheet with a low rim, light enough to carry to the sofa and stiff enough not to flex under a full teapot. Keys, letters and loose change collect on it by the door, and the house is tidier for having one place where things are allowed to be untidy.

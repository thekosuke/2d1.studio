Three blocks of wood and eight wheels. There is no track, no sound, and nothing to switch on, so children supply all of it themselves. The magnets between the cars are weak enough for a small hand to pull apart and strong enough to hold the train together on a trip across the kitchen floor.

It is simple enough to be understood at once and open enough to become something else tomorrow. Good toys, like good tools, leave the interesting part to you.

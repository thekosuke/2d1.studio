Bought secondhand, already broken in by someone we will never meet. Work jackets are designed around a list of jobs: a pocket for a pencil, a pocket for a folding rule, a collar that turns up in the rain. Most people who wear them now have none of those jobs, but the list still shows, and it makes the jacket easy to understand.

The canvas starts stiff and gives in slowly. The corners of the pockets fade first, then the elbows, then the collar. After thirty years it has settled into the shape of whoever wears it, which no new jacket can offer.

2D1
A Design House

An independent design practice by Kosuke Futsukaichi

2D1 is a design house: a place to work with clients, make things of my own, and collect and share the everyday objects I think of as Familiar Characters.

Those interests take the shape of a studio, a shop, and a curated collection, but they all come from the same habit of being particular about the things I surround myself with.

Studio

Clear ideas, with character.

I work with founders and small businesses on visual identities, websites, and digital products. We start by figuring out what you’re trying to do and what’s getting in the way, then give the idea a form people can understand and connect with.

I call my approach reduction with wit: remove what gets in the way, keep what gives it character. I can work with you from the first conversation to the finished design, or focus on the part where you need me.

Shop

Everyday things, made my way.

Sometimes I see an everyday object and wish its shape, material, or the way it works were a little different. The shop is where I get to follow that thought and make my own version.

I’m starting with Life Tote, a canvas bag made in Japan. It’s an ordinary thing with plenty to care about: how it opens, what fits inside, and how it feels after a year of carrying it. The hope is that it becomes someone’s favorite something.

Finds

Good things, made by others.

Finds is a collection of everyday objects I keep coming back to. A mug, a pen, a shoehorn—things with a clear point of view and a little wit. I hand-pick every one, and personally wish I owned everything here.

I think of them as Familiar Characters. Something about them catches your eye, but it’s living with them that gives them meaning. Sharing them is another way to show what I value in design, through the things themselves.

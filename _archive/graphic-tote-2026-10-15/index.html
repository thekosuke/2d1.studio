Illustration of Today Bag in dark canvas, with a red handle and tag and beige 2D1 logo. Inside: a notebook, a bottle held by a short band, and earbuds and keys contained in a square pocket.

Today Bag is our first everyday item: a canvas tote made to tag along wherever you go. A simple shape, handy inner pockets, and a flash of red so you know it’s yours.

Specifications

Material
Tightly woven black canvas.
Origin
Made in Japan
Dimensions
Approx. W 40 × H 36 × D 10 cm, excluding the strap.
Strap
A dark shoulder strap and a short red hand handle.
Red tag
A square woven label, folded into the side seam.
Pockets
A bottle-holding band and a square pocket for small items.
Opening
Magnetic closure sewn into the top hem.
Details
Subtle charcoal pixel eyes on the front; a small beige embroidered 2D1 logo centered near the top of the back.

Prototype specifications. Dimensions and details may change before production.

# Inside the cube — interactive About study

Open http://localhost:4173/_experiments/cube-rooms/ with the usual preview server.

The room stays flat-on, with its outline and corner lines drawn in two dimensions. Native scroll slides Studio out to the right while Shop enters from the left; there is no camera zoom, rotation, or scroll interception. Side walls are bare. Titles occupy the left column and the lead, body copy and CTA occupy the right. Numbered room labels are removed.

Rounded outlines, hatching and the dark/light palette derive from the toy-block house. The desktop title edge follows the shared social-links alignment; mobile retains two columns with a 24px left inset. Reduced motion becomes two static sections, and the HTML remains readable without JavaScript.

This is an English concept prototype, separate from the active bilingual About page. Copy is condensed for the study. Desktop 1440×1000 and phone 390×844 were visually inspected; room buttons and native keyboard scrolling were exercised. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript fallbacks are implemented but not browser-tested in this pass. Visual proofs are in previews/.

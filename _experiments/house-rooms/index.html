What’s inside
a familiar shape?

Three studies for entering Studio and Shop. The house becomes a place you look into, open, or move through.

01 / Open face
Recommended

Remove the front of the block. Its remaining faces become the ceiling, wall, and floor. Work hangs inside Studio; products sit inside Shop. You’re looking into a little world, rather than at another banner.

Enter through the missing face. The room’s depth gently flattens as the copy becomes readable. Move sideways through the shared wall to reach Shop.

Keep: one cavity, one image, one clear idea. Removed: decorative room labels and the display shelf. Leave out furniture, realistic lighting, and tiny objects.

The edit

Keep the open face. Borrow the sideways passage from “Through the wall.” Let the block’s thickness be the one surprising detail; keep the writing flat, legible, and aligned.

These are working studies, not changes to the live About page. “Lean in” previews depth; it is not the final scroll choreography. On phones, the final room would open into a vertical reading layout.

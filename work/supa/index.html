Supa

Overview

Supa takes its cue from “スパッ,” the Japanese sound of a clean cut. That idea runs through an identity built by taking things away: sharp type, simple forms, and a direct voice. Across the website, event stages, and badges, a few clear moves give the brand plenty to say.

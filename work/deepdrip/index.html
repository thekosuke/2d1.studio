DeepDrip

Overview

DeepDrip is an indie editorial platform for developers, imagined as a place to slow down and read over coffee. Drip coffee became a metaphor for its editorial approach: filtering and distilling ideas into something easier to digest. The wordmark’s dripping pixels echo that process, while bright orange cuts through warm coffee tones, giving the space an inviting, slightly nerdy character. The identity carries through a website designed for deeper reading.

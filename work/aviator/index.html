Aviator

Overview

Aviator uses technology to rethink senior healthcare. For its identity and website, we wanted that approach to feel trustworthy and forward-looking.

We drew a mark that reads as both a rising A and the tail of a plane. The shape gives the brand a sense of motion, and a soft purple palette brings a little wonder to it. We carried that balance through to the website.
